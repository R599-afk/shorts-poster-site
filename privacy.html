Shorts Poster

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Shorts Poster is a personal tool, made and used by R599, that uploads its owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what it handles and where that goes.

Who uses it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to, reads or posts to anyone else's account.

What it handles

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, given by each platform's official login.
  • Basic account details that each platform returns when signing in: the channel or account name and its ID. They are used only to show which account is connected and where to post.
  • The owner's videos and captions, which it sends to the platform the owner chooses to post them on.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Shorts Poster has no server and no database, uses no analytics, cookies or advertising, and does not sell or share data with anyone. A video and its caption leave the computer only to be uploaded to the platform the owner chose. For Instagram, the video is briefly made available at a temporary, unlisted web address while Instagram downloads it, and that address closes right afterwards.

Other people's data

Shorts Poster does not collect, read or store information about viewers, followers, commenters or any other users of YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.

Removing access and data

The owner can disconnect Shorts Poster at any time from each platform's settings (Google account permissions, TikTok's Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, and Instagram's Settings > Apps and websites) and can delete its saved tokens by deleting the tool's files.

The platforms' own policies

Uploads are handled by YouTube, TikTok and Instagram under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy, the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy. Shorts Poster's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Children

Shorts Poster is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/R599-afk/shorts-poster-site.
