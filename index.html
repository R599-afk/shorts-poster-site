Shorts Poster

One video, posted to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram

Shorts Poster is a small personal tool made by R599. It runs on its owner's own computer and uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, so each video doesn't have to be uploaded three times by hand.

It is used for two channels run by the same person: the Roblox game Crack a Geode: Shatter or Shine and the science channel 8-Bit Biology.

What it does

  • The owner picks a finished video and its caption, then chooses where to post it.
  • It signs in to each platform through that platform's official login and uploads the video through its official API.
  • It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up for it, and it never posts to anyone else's account.

Your data

Shorts Poster has no users besides its owner and keeps no data about anyone else. Read the privacy policy and the terms of service.
